WHEN A PROFILE FIELD STAYS EMPTY
Image: when-a-profile-field-stays-empty.png

A blank bio is not a missing account.

PROBE keeps account evidence separate from public profile detail. An unavailable name, location, or follower count is not proof of absence—and a displayed value is not proof of identity.
