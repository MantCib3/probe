About PROBE

Public clues.
Clearer context.

PROBE is an open-source intelligence tool for finding where a public username appears online. It checks 377 sources across social networks, developer communities, forums, gaming platforms, marketplaces, and the wider web.

We treat every result as a lead, not proof of identity. Clear evidence, visible uncertainty, and manual verification matter more than a confident-looking guess.
