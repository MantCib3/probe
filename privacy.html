Privacy & Acceptable Use

PROBE searches publicly reachable services on your behalf. Search terms may be transmitted to those services and may appear in infrastructure logs operated by PROBE, its hosting providers, or the destination service.

Information handling

Case notes and pinned links are stored only in your browser's local storage unless you export or submit them. Contact messages and accuracy reports are retained to respond to requests and improve source quality. Do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, or other secrets.

Acceptable use

Use PROBE only for lawful research and investigations. Respect privacy, platform terms, access controls, and applicable law. Do not use the service for harassment, stalking, unauthorized access, or bulk collection of personal data.

Requests

Use the contact form for privacy questions, deletion requests, or responsible security reports.
